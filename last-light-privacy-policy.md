# Privacy Policy for Last Light

**Effective date:** October 7, 2026

Last Light Studio ("we", "us", or "our") provides the mobile game **Last Light** for Android.

**Android package:** `com.lastlightstudio.lastlight`

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Last Light.

## 1. Information processed by the game

Last Light does not require a separate Last Light Studio account and we do not operate our own user-profile database.

The game stores gameplay information such as save data, progression, settings, unlocked content, resources, and other game-state information. This information may be stored locally on your device.

## 2. Google Play and Google Play Games Services

Last Light is distributed through Google Play and may use Google Play Games Services for supported platform features such as sign-in, achievements, and cloud/saved-game functionality.

When you use Google Play or Google Play Games Services, Google may process information such as your Google Play Games identity, game activity, achievements, saved-game data, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, diagnostic information, and other technical data required to provide and secure those services.

Information processed by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and account settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Save data and cloud features

Gameplay data may be stored locally on your device.

If Google Play Games cloud or saved-game functionality is available and enabled, gameplay progress may also be processed by Google Play Games Services so it can be synchronized, backed up, or restored on supported devices.

Last Light Studio does not operate a separate cloud database for Google Play Games save data.

## 4. Purchases

Last Light may be distributed as a paid application through Google Play.

Payments and purchase transactions are processed by Google Play. Last Light Studio does not receive or store your full payment-card or banking details.

Purchase and transaction information may be processed and retained by Google according to Google's applicable policies.

## 5. Advertising

Last Light does not currently contain third-party advertising and does not use advertising SDKs for personalized or behavioral advertising.

## 6. Analytics and diagnostics

Last Light Studio does not currently operate a separate advertising-tracking or user-profiling system for Last Light.

Google Play and Google Play Games Services may process technical, diagnostic, security, and usage information as necessary to provide, protect, troubleshoot, and improve their services.

## 7. How information is used

Information processed through Last Light or supported Google services may be used to:

- provide game functionality;
- save and restore gameplay progress;
- synchronize supported game data;
- provide achievements or other platform features;
- maintain settings and preferences;
- process purchases;
- diagnose technical problems;
- maintain security and prevent abuse.

Last Light Studio does not sell players' personal information.

## 8. Sharing of information

Last Light Studio does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate and distribute the game, principally Google LLC through Google Play and Google Play Games Services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is removed by the game, cleared through Android settings, or otherwise deleted by you.

Data processed through Google Play or Google Play Games Services is retained according to Google's policies and your Google Account settings.

You can manage or delete relevant Google Play Games data through your Google Account and Google Play Games settings.

For privacy or deletion questions specifically relating to Last Light, contact:

**lastlightstudiodev@gmail.com**

## 10. Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.

Last Light does not require a separate Last Light Studio password and does not send your Google account password to a Last Light Studio server.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Children's privacy

Last Light is not specifically directed to children under the minimum age required to independently consent to the processing of personal data in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly operate a separate system designed to collect personal information from children.

## 12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal data, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or data portability.

Where information is controlled directly by Google, those rights should be exercised through your Google Account or Google's privacy controls.

For information controlled directly by Last Light Studio, contact us at **lastlightstudiodev@gmail.com**.

## 13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its services, or applicable legal requirements change.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
