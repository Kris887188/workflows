# Last Light — Data Deletion Request

**Developer:** Last Light Studio  
**Application:** Last Light  
**Android package:** `com.lastlightstudio.lastlight`

Last Light Studio does not operate a separate player-account database.

## How to request deletion

To request deletion of any personal data directly held by Last Light Studio in connection with Last Light:

1. Send an email to **lastlightstudiodev@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject line: **Last Light data deletion request**.
3. State that you want personal data associated with your use of Last Light deleted.
4. If needed, include enough information for us to identify the relevant support correspondence or request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive credentials.

We will review the request and delete applicable data directly controlled by Last Light Studio, unless retention is required by law.

## Data that may be deleted

Depending on your interaction with us, this may include:

- support emails or messages you sent to Last Light Studio;
- technical information you voluntarily provided when contacting support;
- other personal information directly provided to Last Light Studio for support purposes.

Last Light Studio does not maintain a separate in-game account database containing Google account credentials, payment-card details, or Google Play Games profile data.

## Google Play and Google Play Games data

Some data used by Last Light is processed by Google through Google Play and Google Play Games Services, including supported game profile, achievement, purchase, device, and saved-game information.

Data controlled by Google must be managed or deleted through your Google Account, Google Play, or Google Play Games privacy controls.

Google privacy controls: https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Retention

Data directly controlled by Last Light Studio that is subject to a valid deletion request will be deleted within a reasonable period, unless we are required to keep specific information for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Information controlled by Google is retained according to Google's applicable policies and your Google Account settings.
